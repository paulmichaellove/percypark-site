One of the oldest clubs in the region. Twenty two Northumberland Senior Cups, England internationals and British Lions, and a ground at Preston Avenue that the club has owned since 1949.

History

A club with a past, and a ground of its own.

A few of the moments that made Percy Park. Draft from public sources

  1. 1872

    The club is founded by J. Stanley Todd of 60 Percy Park, Tynemouth. It takes its name from his street.

  2. 1896

    Preston Avenue, North Shields becomes home.

  3. 1897

    Tot Robinson plays for England, and goes on to become President of the Rugby Football Union in 1939. Peter Hardwick (1902 to 1904) and Edward Scorfield (1910) follow him into the England side.

  4. 1924

    Carston Catcheside scores a try in each of England’s four Five Nations matches. Bill Wallace becomes the first Percy Park player to go to the British Lions straight from the club.

  5. 1949

    The club buys the Preston Avenue ground for £3,000. It has owned it ever since.

  6. 1960

    Don Rutherford plays full back for England, and tours with the British Lions in 1966. He is the club’s most capped player.

  7. 1986

    The Percy Park Youth Trust is established, on 1 October.

  8. 2018

    A 22nd Northumberland Senior Cup. No club has won it more.

  9. Today

    More than 300 boys and girls play every week, from Under 6 to the 1st XV in Regional 2 North, with the Panthers and the Pirates alongside them.
