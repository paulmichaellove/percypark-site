1st XVPosted 28 October 2026

WPercy Park 31 Durham City 19

Regional 2 North

Victory Over City Brings Fifth Successive Win

As Storm Boelo moved in across the North East, Percy Park entertained Durham City in what was an entertaining and hard fought Regional 2 North game with the swirling wind a factor before the worst of the weather closed in. It was, in many ways, the classic wind affected game of two halves, Park using the conditions in the first half to lead comfortably at the break, City pegging them back to within one score before a late Chris Reekie try secured the win and the bonus point. At the death, Durham pounded the Park line for the score that would have given them both the try and losing bonus points but excellent defence denied them although few in around the ground would have begrudged them something from the game.

1st XVPosted 21 October 2026

WRyton 17 Percy Park 26

Regional 2 North

Park March On After Victory At Ryton

Percy Park maintained their excellent start to the league season with a hard fought win at newly promoted Ryton on Saturday last. In a game dominated by the whistle of Referee Andrew Temple, Park survived the loss of four players to yellow cards to record their fourth successive victory. Indiscipline appeared to be the key ingredient of the match, the home side losing two players to yellow cards, though some decisions clearly perplexed the players and supporters from both sides. That said, it appeared to be a tight, hard but fair contest with Park only securing victory in the last few minutes, former skipper Howard Stock scoring the bonus point try late on.

1st XVPosted 14 October 2026

WNovocastrians 29 Percy Park 38

Regional 2 North

It's Three From Three As Park Defeat Novo's

Park, sitting top of Regional 2 North in the early season table, travelled to Novocastrians, sitting in second, for a top of the table clash last weekend. And in a game dominated by an extremely strong wind it was Park who triumphed through dogged defence against the wind in the first half followed by clever use of it in the second, five second half tries without response ensuring the victory. Incredibly, after their league and cup double last season, this was Novo’s first defeat at Sutherland Park in eighteen months.

1st XVPosted 7 October 2026

WPercy Park 62 Penrith 48

Regional 2 North

Park At The Summit After Two Games

Yes, one hundred and ten points were scored. No, it was not a basketball game. Yes, the defence coaches are not sleeping BUT what an incredible game. Defensive lapses apart (and there were plenty), this was a game full of attacking intent, particularly from Percy Park, and an incredible result against one of the more fancied teams for promotion this year. Credit to Penrith, they played their part in what was a great game, maybe not for the purists because of, on occasion, very poor defence, but for those who truly love the game watching this was pure joy with eighteen tries scored. The visitors, despite a terrible first half, dragged themselves back to within one score in the second before a Park second wind secured the points.

1st XVPosted 28 April 2026

LConsett 59 Percy Park 35

Regional 2 North Play-Off Final

Play-Off Defeat At Consett Ends The Season

Percy Park’s hopes for promotion and the season itself came to an end on Saturday when they suffered defeat at Consett in the Regional 2 North Play-Off Final at Amethyst Park. Four weeks after suffering a high scoring, albeit narrow, league defeat at the same venue, there was a quiet confidence that we could engineer the win against the odds just three days after the County Plate Final defeat to Novocastrians but, after a bright start, a ten minute period before the first quarter had ended saw the home side score four tries that effectively decided the game. From then on, Park were always chasing the game and with a twenty-one-point deficit at the break, there was a mountain to climb. The Consett back line, much like the Novo’s threequarters last Wednesday, were electric and, particularly in that first half, the Park defence had no answers. Consett will now travel to Pocklington, winners of the Regional 2 North East Play-Off Final 34-20 against Morley, next Saturday to decide who plays York (who lost 20-14 at Middlesborough in the Regional 1 North Relegation Play-Off) for the right to play at Level 5 next season.

Novo's Win Plate Final After Second Half Park Collapse

WPercy Park 59 West Hartlepool 41

Regional 2 North

Play-Off Place Secured After Victory Over West

Fourth placed Park took on fifth placed West Hartlepool last weekend, a place in the league play-offs guaranteed for the winner and, in an excellent, high scoring game it was Park who came out victorious after a hard fought and entertaining contest. However, on the Monday after a wonderful day at Preston Avenue, an exciting game preceded by a very well attended past players lunch, there was a sense of anti-climax when Keswick, who finished in fourth place after defeat at Upper Eden, and who we would have faced at home in the Regional 2 North semi-final play-off announced that they were conceding the tie. We will now play the winner of the Consett vs Sunderland semi-final which will be played this coming weekend, 18 April.
