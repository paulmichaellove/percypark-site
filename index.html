Hire the clubhouse

Room for 350, with the pitch outside the window.

Weddings, birthdays, christenings and wakes, all under one roof. Two bars, a private balcony over the pitch, a lift to the upper room and parking on the doorstep. Come and have a look round.

  • 350guests. 200 seated at round tables or 300 at long tables
  • 2 barsfully stocked, so no queue at the door
  • Freeparking on site
