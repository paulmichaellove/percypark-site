Never played

New to rugby?

These are the questions parents and new players ask us most. If yours is not here, ring 0191 257 5710.

Do I need to have played before?

No. Most people who start with us have never played. The Panthers and the girls’ section both say no experience needed, and touch rugby with the Pirates is built for beginners.

What does my child wear?

Old sports kit, boots or trainers, a drink and warm layers. [Gumshield and boots advice by age group, club to confirm]

Is it safe?

Rugby is a contact sport and we coach it with care. [How the youngest sections build up to contact, club to confirm. Coaches checked and first aid cover, club to confirm]

What does it cost?

Your first session is free, so you can try before you pay. After that, [season subs for each section]. Ask us if cost is a worry.

Do I have to stay?

You are welcome to stay and watch. There is free parking, and upstairs there is a bar and a balcony looking over the pitch. Or drop them off and come back.

What if my child is nervous?

Tell the coach at the door and they will look after them. They can bring a friend. [A named friendly face to greet new players, club to confirm]

Players: season gym access.

If you play for Percy Park, you can use the club gym for the whole season. It costs £30, once.

  • You pay once, at the start of the season, on ClubZap. The item is called Gym Access.
  • It is in or out. Pay it and you are in for the season. Do not pay it and you are not.
  • It is for players only. It is not a public gym membership.
  • There is nothing monthly. It is separate from your playing subs and from club membership.
